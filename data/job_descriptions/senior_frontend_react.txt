Senior Frontend Engineer — Consumer Fintech Platform
NorthBridge Financial | Remote (US timezones) | Full-time

About the role
NorthBridge is rebuilding our consumer investing app on a new frontend
platform. We're looking for a Senior Frontend Engineer to help lead that
rebuild — someone who can own features end-to-end, make solid architectural
calls, and write code that a regulated fintech product can depend on.

Requirements
- 3+ years of professional experience building production React applications
- Strong proficiency in TypeScript in a production codebase
- Experience with a state management approach (Redux, Redux Toolkit, Zustand,
  or equivalent)
- Solid HTML5/CSS3 fundamentals and experience with responsive, cross-browser
  UI
- Experience writing automated frontend tests (Jest, React Testing Library,
  Cypress, or similar)
- Comfortable working directly with designers and product managers to shape
  requirements, not just implement a spec
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience

Nice to have
- Experience with Next.js or another React SSR/meta-framework
- Experience with GraphQL clients (Apollo, urql, or similar)
- Prior experience in fintech, banking, or another regulated industry
- Familiarity with accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1 AA)
- Experience mentoring other engineers or leading small feature teams
- Performance optimization experience (bundle size, Core Web Vitals)

What success looks like in the first 6 months
- Shipped at least one major feature end-to-end in the new platform
- Raised the bar on test coverage and code review quality for the frontend
  team
- Comfortable being the go-to person for frontend architecture questions
